Welcome to Cinnamoroll

Cinnamoroll, also called Cinnamon or "Cinna" was born on March 6 on a cloud high in the sky. He lives at Café Cinnamon, where he spreads joy, sometimes taking naps on customers' laps. He enjoys warm, fresh cinnamon rolls, fittingly reflecting his name. His adventures often involve his friends, including Chiffon, Mocha, Espresso, Cappuccino, and Milk, as well as his rival duo, Lloromannic. These characters each have unique personalities, making the Cinnamoroll universe rich and engaging.
